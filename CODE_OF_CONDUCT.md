# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Clear bug reports and reproduction steps.
- Good-faith feature suggestions.
- Respectful disagreement.
- Contributions that keep the project legal, safe, and useful.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language.
- Requests or contributions intended for infringement, credential misuse, DRM bypass, paywall bypass, or access-control circumvention.
- Sharing private links, secrets, cookies, or credentials in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues, reject pull requests, remove comments, or block participants who violate these standards.
